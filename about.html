About [Site Name]

[Site Name] is a directory of public, municipal, resort and private golf courses. It shows raw, checkable data in a dense, sortable table: holes, par, yardage, course rating, slope, elevation, location, course type, and published rankings. We are starting with Colorado and plan to add other states over time.

Objective data, no opinions

We don't assign our own scores, star ratings or "difficulty" grades. Every value comes from a named source or a direct measurement. When a value can't be verified, it is left blank rather than estimated. Where a ranking appears, it belongs to the publication that issued it, not to us.

Data sources

  • Course data (names, locations, tees, par, yardage, rating and slope where available): OpenGolfAPI, made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0. Contains information from OpenGolfAPI, which is made available under the ODbL. We corrected, merged and supplemented some records.
  • Rankings: Golf Digest Best in State: Colorado (2025-26) and America's 100 Greatest Golf Courses (2025-26). Ranks are cited with links. Golf Digest is not affiliated with this site.
  • Elevation: Open-Meteo elevation API.
  • Photos: Wikimedia Commons contributors, credited under each image according to its license.
  • Architect links: Wikipedia.
  • Some city names: © OpenStreetMap contributors (Nominatim).

Popular badge

The Popular badge marks up to 10 publicly playable courses per state, based on our research into online popularity. It is never sold and is not influenced by payment.
