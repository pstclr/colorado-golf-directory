This policy explains what information [Site Name] ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short, we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't sell your personal information.

Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix data issues you report.

When you visit, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and operating purposes. We don't control those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Information stored in your browser. The site may save your table settings, such as sort order and filters, in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics (future). We don't run analytics today. We may later add a privacy-focused analytics tool to count visits and clicks on Book links, for example to measure referrals to booking partners. If we do, we'll update this policy, name the provider, explain whether it uses cookies, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

2. Cookies

We don't set first-party cookies today. If that changes, this section will list each cookie and its purpose.

Affiliate cookies. When you click an affiliate Book link, for example to GolfNow or TeeOff, or through an affiliate network such as FlexOffers, that partner or network may set a cookie or use a tracking parameter on its own site. This records that you came from us, so we can be credited if you book. Those cookies follow the partner's privacy policy, not ours. You can block or delete them in your browser settings.

If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with "Appeal" in the subject line. If you're not satisfied with the outcome of your appeal, you can contact the Colorado Attorney General.
