Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated:

The short version: [Site Name] may earn money when you click some links and make a booking or purchase. This never costs you extra, and it never changes our data, rankings or badges.

Book buttons

Some "Book" buttons go to third-party tee-time services such as GolfNow or TeeOff. Those links may be affiliate links. If you book through them, we may earn a commission from that service at no extra cost to you. Other Book buttons go straight to a course's own tee-time page. We usually earn nothing from those, although the booking system behind a course page may be run by a company we have an affiliate relationship with.

Sponsor banner

The banner at the top of the directory is paid advertising. Sponsors pay for that placement. A sponsor does not get better data, rankings or placement in the table.

Gear and other links

We may occasionally link to golf equipment or other products through affiliate programs. Those links may earn us a commission too.

What payment never affects

  • Golf Digest rankings, which are cited from Golf Digest exactly as published.
  • Tournament history, which comes from cited public records. Courses can't buy it.
  • Course data such as par, yardage, rating, slope and elevation.
  • Default sort order and filters.
